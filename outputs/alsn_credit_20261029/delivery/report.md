- Sales rose 6.26% from USD 3,035m in FY2023 to 3,225m in FY2024, then fell 6.67% to 3,010m in FY2025. Management attributes the 2025 decline principally to weaker North American medium-duty and Class 8 vocational truck demand and weaker global off-highway demand; defense and overseas on-highway growth partly offset the decline.
- Gross margin was 48.43%, 47.41% and 48.60% in FY2023, FY2024 and FY2025. Management attributes the 2025 recovery to pricing, lower incentive compensation and the absence of 2024 UAW signing incentives, partly offset by unfavorable direct material costs.
- Operating profit rose from USD 919m in FY2023 to 992m in FY2024, then fell to 880m in FY2025. The FY2025 operating margin of 29.24% was 1.52 percentage points below FY2024; USD 64m of acquisition expenses and USD 29m of long-lived asset impairments weighed on earnings, partly offset by lower R&D spending.
- 利息費用：尚未完成分析；所選來源尚無相符資料；不代表整份申報未揭露
- Pretax profit was USD 827m, 897m and 804m in FY2023–FY2025. FY2025 net income fell 14.77% to USD 623m from 731m; lower operating earnings and a higher tax charge outweighed the improvement in other income.

- Current ratios increased from 2.50x in FY2023 to 3.04x in FY2024 and 4.85x in FY2025. Year-end 2025 cash of USD 1,495m and unused revolving capacity of USD 745m preceded the January 2026 acquisition; these balances should not be treated as post-acquisition liquidity.
- CFO increased from USD 784m in FY2023 to 801m in FY2024 and 836m in FY2025. Management attributes the latest increase to lower cash taxes and working-capital funding needs, the absence of prior-year UAW signing incentives and lower pension funding, partly offset by weaker gross profit and acquisition expenses.
- Total liabilities divided by consolidated equity decreased from 307.54% in FY2023 to 223.20% in FY2024, then rose to 225.76% in FY2025. This metric measures liabilities relative to equity and differs from debt/EBITDA.
